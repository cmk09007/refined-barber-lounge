What is Refined Barber Lounge & Cafe?

Refined Barber Lounge & Cafe is a barbershop at 8424 N. Nebraska Ave in North Tampa, FL 33604. Two barbers, Gio and Darienne, each keep their own chair and their own calendar; walk-ins are welcome and a cafe is coming soon.

Cuts, fades, shape-ups and scissor cuts, beards and steamed-towel neck shaves, kids’ cuts, long hair, waxing, scalp treatments and color.
