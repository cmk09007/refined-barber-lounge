Is there parking?

Yes — parking is free. There is a lot at the shop, and street parking on N. Nebraska Ave right outside it. Nothing to pay, nothing to validate. The entrance is wheelchair accessible and so is the restroom.

How do I get to Refined Barber Lounge & Cafe?

Head for 8424 N. Nebraska Ave, on the east side of Nebraska between Busch Blvd and Sligh Ave. Coming down Busch Blvd, turn onto N. Nebraska Ave and head south. Coming up from Seminole Heights, stay on Nebraska Ave north past Hillsborough Ave.
