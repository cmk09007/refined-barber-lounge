What is included in a cut?

Consultation, cut, finish. With Darienne, a post-cut wash and a steamed-towel neck shave come with it. Shampoo wash and scalp treatment can be added on.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Yes, under 12 and up. Gio books kids at 45 minutes, Darienne at 30. Google flags the shop Good for kids. Kids’ pricing: {{VERIFY:SHOP_WIDE_PRICES}}

Do you cut long hair and women’s hair?

Yes. Darienne cuts long hair below the shoulders and short styles above, and does natural dimension color blending for gray coverage with natural depth.

What about beards, shape-ups, and shaves?

Beard only, shape up, shape up plus beard, haircut plus beard. Beards get thinned, shaped, edged, and cleaned up with a foil shaver. Steamed-towel neck shave with the cut.

Do you do eyebrows, waxing, or scalp treatments?

Yes. Eyebrow shaping and ear, nose, and brow waxing. Scalp treatment, a 10-minute scalp massage, and an add-on facial — purify, exfoliate, detox, hydrate, seal.
