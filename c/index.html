Questions people ask

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome at Refined Barber Lounge & Cafe, and Saturday is walk-in only with Gio. The shop is open Monday through Saturday, 11:00 AM to 6:30 PM, and closed Sunday. Walk in and you take the next open chair; book ahead if you need a specific time or a specific barber.

How do I book an appointment at Refined Barber Lounge & Cafe?

Book with the barber, not the shop. Gio (Giovanni De La Rosa) takes appointments through The Cut app at book.thecut.co/giocutzz. Darienne takes appointments through GlossGenius at refinedbarberlounge.glossgenius.com. Each barber keeps their own calendar, their own hours, and their own menu, so pick your barber first and book them direct. Prefer to call? {{VERIFY:PHONE}}

Is there parking at the shop?

Yes, there is a parking lot at the shop. Google lists wheelchair-accessible parking, a wheelchair-accessible entrance, and a wheelchair-accessible restroom at 8424 N. Nebraska Ave, Tampa, FL 33604. Full parking details, including street parking: {{VERIFY:PARKING}}

How much is a haircut?

Between $40 and $43 for an adult haircut, depending on which barber you book. There is no single shop price list. On Gio's The Cut menu: haircut $43, scissor cut $48, shape up $28, haircut and beard $58. On Darienne's GlossGenius menu: haircut from $40, long hair from $40, haircut and beard from $55. What are your hours?

Monday through Saturday, 11:00 AM to 6:30 PM. Closed Sunday. Those are the shop hours listed on the Google Business Profile. A barber's own chair hours can be narrower than the shop's, so check their booking page: Darienne books Tuesday through Saturday, 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes. Both barbers list kids' cuts, and Google flags the shop Good for kids. Gio books a kids' cut at 45 minutes; Darienne books under-12 cuts at 30 minutes. Families are a regular part of the room, not an exception. Kids' pricing: {{VERIFY:SHOP_WIDE_PRICES}}

Do you cut women's hair and long hair?

Yes. Darienne's menu covers long hair below the shoulders, short styles above the shoulders, and natural dimension color blending for gray coverage. Every cut with her includes a consultation, a post-cut wash, and a steamed-towel neck shave. Book her through GlossGenius.

Do you do beard trims and shaves?

Yes. Gio's menu lists beard only, shape up plus beard, and haircut plus beard. Darienne's beard service is a clean-up: thinned and shaped, edges and lines cleaned, foil shaver over the stubble, plus a steamed-towel neck shave with any haircut. Beard pricing: {{VERIFY:SHOP_WIDE_PRICES}}
